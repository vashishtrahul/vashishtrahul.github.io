Hi, I’m **Rahul**, a PhD student at the [Department of Computer Science](https://www.cse.iitm.ac.in/index.php), [IIT Madras](https://www.iitm.ac.in/). I work under the guidance of [Dr. Harish G. Ramaswamy](https://sites.google.com/site/harishguruprasad/home?authuser=0), focusing on the **Theory of Attention Mechanisms in Deep Learning** from a **Learning Dynamics** perspective.

My PhD thesis, titled _"Attention Mechanisms: Learning Dynamics and Interpretability,"_ investigates how attention distributions and output probability distributions **emerge**, **evolve**, and **specialize** during training. I also study the trajectory of learned parameters to better understand their dynamics over time. <!---I am fortunate to be supported by the **HTRA Fellowship for Doctoral Research**.-->

During my internship with the **Last Mile team at Amazon India**, I explored a novel adaptation of **semi-supervised learning** for training language models to perform **address matching** — a task crucial for applications such as delivery services, online retail, and emergency response systems.
